# 역할별 초상화 업데이트

내장 Imagegen으로 8개 역할을 각각 독립 생성했습니다. 원본 PNG는 `frontend/public/assets/roles/`에 포함됩니다.
역할 도감, 나의 역할 공개, 게임 종료 결과에 적용했습니다. 공개 참가자 좌석은 공통 이미지를 유지합니다.
이름 숨김 상태에서는 초상화도 렌더링하지 않습니다. 모바일에서도 세로 비율을 유지합니다.

## 생성 프롬프트

공통: Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE original standalone role portrait for an Arthurian dark-fantasy social deduction game. Vertical 2:3 composition, detailed cinematic painterly realistic fantasy illustration, consistent premium collectible card-art series. Chest-up central character, complete head and headwear inside image, face in upper middle, readable silhouette at small size. Deep charcoal background, restrained antique gold accents, dramatic soft chiaroscuro, richly textured fabrics and metals. Image fills canvas. No border, text, letters, labels, watermark, UI or collage. Distinct original character design.

### merlin.png

Merlin, elderly wise male seer with long silver beard, weathered face, midnight-blue embroidered robes, holding an ancient oak staff crowned with blue starlight, astrolabe behind him.

### percival.png

Percival, young clean-shaven male knight with curly auburn hair, ivory and sapphire armor, white cloak, holding a silver grail emitting soft light, searching thoughtful gaze.

### servant.png

Loyal servant of Arthur, resolute female royal guard with braided brown hair, practical polished silver armor and royal blue tabard, simple golden lion clasp, upright broad sword and shield, warm daylight.

### assassin.png

Assassin, lean masked figure in deep charcoal hood and black leather, only intense eyes visible, one elegant dagger held downward near chest, crimson rim light, stealthy quiet dangerous silhouette, no gore.

### morgana.png

Morgana, regal adult sorceress with long raven hair, dark plum velvet gown and ornate silver crescent diadem, holding an enchanted mirror with violet mist, cool calculating expression.

### mordred.png

Mordred, imposing middle-aged male dark king with short black beard, jagged black iron crown and heavy obsidian armor with antique gold engraving, deep burgundy cloak, proud severe expression, ember lit throne chamber.

### oberon.png

Oberon, mysterious solitary fae king with long pale hair and pointed ears, branching antler crown, moss green ancient bark armor, emerald woodland fireflies, uncanny amber eyes, foggy forest backdrop.

### minion.png

Minion of Mordred, scarred bald rugged male war veteran with one cloudy eye, battered bronze and black chainmail, torn rust-red cloak, heavy iron mace resting on shoulder, bleak smoky fortress backdrop.
